Challenge/template: https://dev.to/challenges/hacktoberfest-weekend-2026-10-01

1. Identify one real friend or loved one and their actual problem. Personalize the
   first section of DEV_SUBMISSION.md. A composite research profile does not satisfy
   this requirement by itself. Do not invent feedback.
2. Try the running app. If possible, let that person use it and record their actual
   reaction. Label any untested part honestly.
3. Put this project's source in a new public repository created within the challenge
   window. Keep dependencies, credentials, personal documents, and private logs out.
4. Upload the prepared MP4 to YouTube (public/unlisted), Vimeo, or another accessible
   video host. Use the public video URL in Demo; do not use localhost.
5. Open the official Submission Template on the challenge page. Paste the final
   article, upload relevant screenshots, and keep all three tags:
   devchallenge, weekendchallenge, hf26challenge.
6. Replace every bracketed drafting note with verified details or remove the optional
   section. Preview the article. Test the code and video links while signed out.
7. Publish the one completed entry before the deadline. Verify the published URL and
   required tags. Only the first entry is considered if multiple entries are made.
8. Check the linked DEV account's Hacktoberfest activity separately; do not infer
   sticker credit solely from article publication.
